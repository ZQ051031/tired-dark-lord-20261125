A make.fun entry for [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb): one speech, about a hundred words, from a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

## The speech

Forty years into the same siege, the dark lord receives the latest hero in
his throne room. The menace is real — the river gate opens at dawn by his
own arrangement, and the city will kneel, as cities do — but the man
delivering it keeps drifting. A chair that bites. Generals bringing maps he
no longer reads. Candles that need trimming, right in the middle of a death
threat. He never once says he is weary; the weariness is in the rhythm, in
sentences that trail off and restart somewhere else. The sting lands last:
he has a speech prepared for this moment. He wrote it down. Somewhere.

## Running it

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library and writes the monologue to
`/output/entry.txt`. The output is fixed — run it as many times as you like
and the bytes never change:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The entry is exactly 100 words and 547 characters, with no trailing
newline.
